AI-use policies for clinical and biomedical informatics venues cascade through four layers. Each layer inherits the one above and adds its own requirements, and in a conflict the strictest applicable layer binds you. Select any node to trace its lineage and see what that layer adds.

The common floor · every venue surveyed agrees
  1. No AI authorsAI can't take responsibility, declare conflicts or hold copyright (COPE's reasoning).
  2. Humans are accountableIncluding fabricated references, plagiarism and errors in AI output.
  3. Research use goes in MethodsDescribed well enough to reproduce.
  4. Hiding it is misconductUndisclosed substantive use can lead to rejection, correction or retraction.
  5. No public tools for reviewReviewers may not upload confidential manuscripts to unsecured AI.
